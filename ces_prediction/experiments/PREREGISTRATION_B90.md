Paper questions 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5: compare direct causal nowcasting models against the same history-only controls, quantify the fast-channel increment, temporal transfer and CPU step latency. A standard regressor is an eligible estimator; neither new architecture nor physical discovery is required or claimed. This batch does not establish a new physical mechanism. B.88 separately tests explicit measurement-time anchors. The shortlist here implements every other direction in method_selection_20261122.md's five priorities; it does not purport to implement every overlapping item in the literature survey.

Freeze and reuse B.85 real D1 grid/labels/22 inputs, train-only normalization, 15 split units (V1 r2 five folds, V2 s1 five folds, V3 q1..4, V4a), train/validation/test manifests, seven training/validation availability rules and K10 test-only rule. F and L5 are sensor-derived targets, not independent physical truth. State resets at each original segment. X reads all22 channels; paired H zeros first15 just as B.85.
