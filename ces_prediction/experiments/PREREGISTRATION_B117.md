Registered 2026-11-27 before any B117 optimization. The author explicitly asked to investigate one model deeply instead of dividing short training budgets across architectures. B116 is complete and frozen. This direction is informed by its previously inspected TEST results and training histories; this pilot is exploratory and does not restore an untouched TEST set.

## Sufficient optimization and honest termination

Start from the same random initialization and repeat the original rule assignment and batch order, AdamW lr.001/weightdecay.0001/clip1 and ReduceLROnPlateau patience3/factor.5/minlr1e-5/default relative threshold1e-4. This is a budget extension with validation-driven termination, not a new architecture or a claim that more epochs necessarily improve TEST error. Preserve the earliest strict minimum physical equal-seven-rule common-support VAL MSE as the best model.

There is NO fifteen-minute cap. A normal convergence stop requires ALL of:

- at least fifty complete TRAIN-plus-VAL epochs;
- at least ten consecutive complete epochs actually using lr<=1e-5;
- at least twenty complete epochs since a significant VAL improvement, defined prospectively as an improvement of at least0.1% relative to the last significant best (score < significant_best*0.999). Initialize significant_best at the first validated score. This criterion is separate from strict checkpoint selection.

Stop immediately after the first epoch satisfying all conditions. Report this as an operational VAL plateau criterion, not mathematical convergence or guaranteed optimality. Two safety boundaries are maximum200 epochs or six hours of fit time; reaching either before the plateau criterion means SAFETY_CAPPED_NOT_CONVERGED and must not be presented as completed convergence. Keep any completed validated checkpoints and explicitly record partial updates/epochs. Fit time includes batching/TRAIN/VAL; a partial epoch is excluded from selected checkpoints. An owned workflow may allocate up to eight hours from initial launch for waiting/training and one further hour for CPU analysis/audit/publication; do not reset the clock, retry failed fits, or stop another process. These are maximum safety allocations, not completion-time promises.

Persist best weights at EVERY strict VAL improvement, and persist a recoverable latest validated checkpoint after EVERY complete epoch, including model/optimizer/scheduler state, next epoch, RNG states and stopping counters. Write atomically and record hashes. The latest recoverable state is distinct from the selected best state; do not silently resume from best with a reset optimizer. No automatic fit retry or scientific resumption is authorized by this registration; a future author pause may preserve the checkpoint for a separately recorded continuation. Emit batch/epoch phase heartbeats and losses, LR, update counts, timing, best epoch and stopping counters so actual progress is observable.
