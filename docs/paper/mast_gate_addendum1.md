## 7. Notes

- The first run is the registered primary; this addendum is reported next to it and does not replace it. The TEST tables and the TUNE surfaces of the first run had been seen when the addendum was registered.
- The pipeline is `run_gate.main()` itself: the addendum replaces the grid constants read by `run_gate.tune()` and wraps it in the stopping rule; `run_gate.py` is not edited.
- Population W uses the hyperparameters tuned on population G and the best causal arm chosen in population G, as in the first run.
- The JSON key `retuned` holds the complete output of the pipeline with the retuned arms (same structure as `mast_gate.json`); its `expectations` entry is section 6 of the FIRST registration re-evaluated on the retuned arms, and its `deviations_ambiguities` entry is the first-run list, which applies unchanged except for the grids.
- 'Last step' changes are TUNE quantities. An edge-bound hyperparameter means the pooled TUNE MSE has no interior minimum inside the allowed extensions; the skill of that family is then a lower bound for the family only up to the reported last-step change.
- V_i offline GP: the selected point (640 ms, 2048 x scale) lies on a flat ridge of the TUNE surface where length and amplitude grow together (pooled TUNE MSE 19.27 to 19.60 for every length from 7.07 ms to 320 ms at that amplitude); the amplitude is edge-bound after the two allowed extensions with a last-step change of -1.5 %.
- A first execution of this script started the V_i tuning from the grids T_i had ended with (the starting grids were read from module globals already replaced), which is not the registered procedure; its TEST output was printed before the defect was found. Fixed by capturing the first-run grids at import; every number here is from the corrected run. The T_i numbers of the two executions are identical; for V_i the filter and causal-GP selections are identical (158.5 / 10; 40 ms, 16) and the offline-GP selection differs (160 ms, 256 then; 640 ms, 2048 now, one more extension round).
