# MAST fast-signal exploration -- discovery half, exploratory tables (2026-11-16)

Protocol: `ces_prediction/experiments/MAST_FAST_EXPLORATION.md`. Code: `ces_prediction/experiments/mast/explore_fast.py`. Every number below comes from the odd-numbered shots of `mast/coupling_shots.txt` (discovery half) and from shot-level 5-fold prediction inside that half. Nothing here is a confirmed result: features, model forms and cells were chosen while looking at these shots. The even-numbered shots have not been opened for this task.

Task: block frames at odd positions are hidden; a hidden 5 ms frame is estimated from charge-exchange values of earlier visible frames and from beam power / mid-plane D-alpha up to the end of the hidden frame window (frame windows are centred on the stored frame time; the exposure convention is not in the metadata).

Reading the tables: a cell `a vs b` is 1 - SSE_a / SSE_b on the rows of the cell, with a 95 % interval from 400 shot-level bootstrap resamples. `rmse pers` is the persistence RMSE (eV for T_i, km/s for V_i). `noise share of pers MSE` is the part of the persistence squared error that the reported errors of the two frames account for. D-alpha step rows: the frame-mean D-alpha changed by at least the 90th percentile of |change| / block median between the last visible frame and the hidden frame. Beam steady: no frame-to-frame change of the SS beam power above 5 % of its shot maximum in the preceding 60 ms and the beam above half of its maximum.
