## 7. Deviations and ambiguities (most literal reading taken)

- Block, scored chords and scored rows are built separately per target and population from that target's good-sample rule (the registration defines the block through 'good chords' without saying which target fixes it).
- Frames are addressed by block position on the nominal 5 ms grid: 't - 5 ms', 't - 15 ms', 't + 5 ms' are block positions j - 1, j - 3, j + 1, and every time-dependent weight (extrapolation, running-mean age, filter dt, GP kernel) uses position x 5 ms. Stored frame times enter the usable-shot rule only. Reason: a few usable shots carry block frame intervals outside 5.0 +- 0.1 ms, some of them negative (time stamps out of order), which the registration did not anticipate; these shots stay in the row set as registered, their count is in the counts table, and section 5b repeats the gate statistics with them dropped.
- Running mean: all good visible samples of the block before the hidden frame, weights exp(-age / tau), normalised to sum 1.
- Slope-state filter: discrete model per frame interval, F = [[1, dt], [0, 1]], Q = diag(q_level dt, q_slope dt) with dt in 5 ms steps; exact diffuse initialisation (level from the first good sample, slope fixed by the second); bad visible samples get no update. Grid (not fixed numerically in the registration): q_level / scale^2 = 10^(-2 .. +1.5), q_slope / scale^2 = 10^(-4 .. +1), 6 log-spaced values each.
- GP window: 'last 8 visible samples' = the 8 visible block frames before the hidden frame (fewer at the block start), offline 4 visible frames on each side; bad samples inside the window are dropped, the window is not extended. Constant-kernel variance = 1e6 x amplitude^2 (not stated in the registration), applied through the Sherman-Morrison identity.
- Target scale for the GP amplitude and the filter grid: median reported error of the hidden sample over TUNE scored rows, population G.
- Population W uses the hyperparameters tuned on population G (the registration tunes on G only) and the best causal arm chosen in population G.
- Gate (a): the chords entering S_k are the scored chords with at least 12 triples at that spacing (the chord set may differ between k). Co-reported ratio: rows = all good d_1 triples of scored chords in TEST shots, population G.
- Regime number: Pearson correlation of consecutive 10 ms increments (both increments from good visible samples), chords with at least 8 such pairs. R2 per-shot skills are 1 - SSE_arm / SSE_persistence within the shot.
- Radius tertiles: edges are the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of major radius over the TEST scored rows of that target and population (pooled over shots).
- Top-0.1 % rows: ceil(0.001 N) rows with the largest squared residual of the best causal arm; the same rows are dropped from every arm. The interval of the share keeps that row set fixed.
- Bootstrap medians over rows are weighted medians with the shot multiplicities (lower median); point estimates use numpy.median. Intervals are percentile intervals (2.5, 97.5).
- Section 6 expectations stated without an interval rule are judged on the point estimate; 'about -0.4' is judged as within +-0.1; 'no better than 0' as the interval not lying above 0.
- If gate (a) passes and the co-reported ratio interval lies below 1, the rescaled floor is the mean reduced chi-square divided by the point estimate of the ratio (interval scaled by the same factor).
- `ss_chisq`: not reported: the column count of ss_chisq equals the chord count in 17 of 264 gate files (chords / ss_chisq columns: 96 / 160, 160 / 160, 160 / 224), so its columns are not mapped to chords.
