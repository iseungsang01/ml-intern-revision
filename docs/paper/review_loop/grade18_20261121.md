## Major concerns as ranked by the grader

1. **The target is not validated on the scale that is scored.** No spectra, amplitudes or fit errors; a single reading deviates from the level of the next five frames with an RMSE of 183 eV at a median of 43 eV, so the MSE is set by a heavy tail; readings of 1.1 and 2.5 keV on a 600 eV level remain targets and the 3 keV cut is never varied; failed fits are three times as frequent at low BES level; after a notch the reported T_i falls to 0.76 to 0.84 of its level. The regression may be learning when the fit degrades. Wanted: re-scoring without rows after a notch and at low BES level, a varied cut, a robust loss, under the designs in time order (new analysis); conclusively, fit errors per frame (new data, no new experiment).
2. **The comparison with the interpolation that waits is not like for like.** The estimator is trained on about 500 discharges; PCHIP and the offline GP are not trained. Learned against unlearned is confounded with causal against two-sided. Wanted: the same boosted regression given a two-sided history, a robust two-sided smoother, and the accuracy against the permitted lag (0, 1, 2, 5 frames), since waiting one or two frames costs 10 to 20 ms (new analysis).
3. **The use scenarios are assumptions, and the realistic one is not robust.** The schedule, the age of 0.65 s and the zero analysis time are constructions; under the schedule the increment is absent on the last block, also under the random design; one fifth of the rolling-origin design is below its size-matched control and the main text did not say so. Wanted: B_T, ECEI settings, I_p, density, equilibria (new data), or the claim restricted to shot ranges with stable behaviour (rewriting).
4. **Co-location and "pedestal top" are not shown.** The chord position is not reconciled with the published one; the BES array moves; the ECEI radius is uncertain by 2 to 3 cm; regimes are pooled; one BES column gives the full BES gain, Mirnov a quarter, and under rolling origin the estimator is not established above the Mirnov estimator. Wanted: the localisation removed from title, abstract and keywords, or equilibria.
5. **Inference.** Percentile bootstrap on 14 to 19 clusters, 147 statements without multiplicity control, intervals without fit variance, features chosen on the scored discharges, no untouched data; "established" for lower ends of 0.1 or 0.7. Wanted: one primary design (chronological), a small corrected primary family, seed variance inside the intervals.
6. **MAST.** Thomson scattering there is not faster than the charge-exchange frames: a same-cadence regression across diagnostics with an imposed delay, on a band defined from the same Thomson profile.

Assessment by criterion, in the grader's words: validation "unusually careful"; "the abstract is largely faithful; Discussion and Conclusion overstate in places"; "it reads as an audit log" (about twenty labels, no profile, no ELM-resolved example, a supplement of 34 tables); novelty of a causal, staleness-resolved evaluation of a slow edge diagnostic against fast ones "as far as my search goes, new", the method standard.

The three changes it ranks highest: (1) fit errors and amplitudes per frame with B_T, ECEI settings and equilibria of the same discharges (new data from the archive); (2) artefact-controlled re-scoring, learned two-sided references and an accuracy-against-lag curve, the chronological design primary (new analysis of existing data); (3) a rewrite around one primary claim with plain names and the registration bookkeeping moved out (rewriting only).
