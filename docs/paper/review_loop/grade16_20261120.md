# Independent grading, round 16 (2026-11-20)

The grader was a separate agent in the role of a PPCF referee. It was given two frozen PDFs only (main text 17 pages, supplement 22 pages, the state of commit ab344d7) and no record, conclusion or earlier grading of this project; it was told not to open the repository.

Result: major revision (borderline with reject and resubmit). PPCF accept 15 %; Fusion Engineering and Design 32 %; Review of Scientific Instruments 27 %; sent to referees 60 %.

## Major concerns as ranked by the grader

1. The gain is not localized, and one confound is untested: after a notch the reported T_i and BES dip; under the schedule rule every scored row follows a notch the history has not seen. Wanted: the increment by time since the last notch, by the size of the fast-level change since the anchor, quiescent against transient phases. (New analysis of existing data.)
2. Squared error on a heavy-tailed quantity; absolute accuracy never stated against the 10 % of the requirement documents. Wanted: MAE, median relative error, quantiles, per-discharge distribution. (New analysis.)
3. The prospective test under the real schedule fails, and non-stationarity is wider than stated (third fifth of the rolling-origin design); "co-located" cannot be verified per discharge. (New data; failing that, rewriting.)
4. The specificity of "same radius" is not tested: Mirnov as a non-co-located control, BES columns separately, inputs swapped between discharges. (New analysis.)
5. Intervals are conditional on one fit and on 14 to 18 test clusters; 99 statements without multiplicity control. (New analysis and rewriting.)
6. Nothing fast is used: one sample per 10 ms row; the result is about present against stale. (Rewriting.)

Clarity was called poor for a journal paper: more than thirty labels, internal batch codes, no displayed equation, definitions deferred to the repository.
