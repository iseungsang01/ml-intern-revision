A fresh agent in the role of a PPCF referee, with the same instructions as round 16, read two frozen PDFs only (main text 20 pages, supplement 25 pages; the state of commit 1ba9a8b, after B.82 had been written in). It had no record, conclusion or earlier grading of this project and was told not to open the repository.

Result: reject and resubmit. PPCF accept 13 %; Fusion Engineering and Design 30 %; Review of Scientific Instruments 22 %; sent to referees 55 %.

Round 16 on the manuscript before B.82: 15 %, 32 %, 27 %, 60 %. The additions of B.82 (robust measures, the increment by time since a notch, the specificity controls, with their limits in the abstract) did not raise the estimates; the grader read the new limits as further reasons.

## Major concerns as ranked by the grader

1. The result fails where it mimics operation: under the schedule rule in the chronological design nothing is established, and in the third fifth of the rolling-origin design fitting on the past is worse than fitting on random discharges; the headline numbers come from a synthetic delay. Wanted: the schedule rule in time order as the primary analysis; a causal recalibration per discharge that survives it; per-shot settings (new data).
2. The target is not validated: L5 is a forward median of the same reported values; every MSE is driven by excursions of unknown origin; after a notch the reported T_i is at 0.76 to 0.84 of its level. Wanted: a noise floor of the target (odd/even split of the five frames), scoring with post-notch frames removed from target and score, a robust loss as primary; spectra and fit errors would settle it (new data).
3. "Co-located" is not supported (Mirnov carries a quarter; neither half holds in all designs; positions uncertain by 2 to 3 cm). Wanted: retitle and restate; baselines from generic state signals (new data).
4. Interval calibration: the permuted control excludes zero in two of ten cells; lower bounds of 3.3 and 6.1 without multiplicity control. Wanted: few-cluster-corrected or permutation-calibrated intervals, seed variance included, one primary family.
5. MAST: errors not monotonic in the delay under the chronological design; no data table and no robust measure.
6. Presentation: one question, one primary endpoint, four or five tables.

The three changes it ranks highest: (1) validate the target and recalibrate the statistics, then state the one result that survives the schedule rule in time order (new analysis); (2) rewrite as a short paper on what a present-time signal is worth as a function of the age of the CES value, without "co-located" in the title, the realistic schedule first in the abstract, at most twelve pages and five tables (rewriting); (3) an untouched later campaign with per-shot settings and fit errors (new data).
