## Operational choices made before the run (where the registration leaves room)

- Population: the registered one (k = 1; hold, GP, twin, causal variant and measurement finite), without the has_future and PCHIP requirement of the sensitivity script (about 100 more rows per split).
- Top 1 %: rows whose hold squared error is at or above its 99th percentile within the split.
- Thirds: tertiles of the last visible sample (hold) within the split.
- Bursts: an absent frame is a 20 to 40 ms step t_j -> t_(j+1) in the 10 ms file; an onset is excluded when it falls in (t_j, t_(j+1) + 90 ms], i.e. on or within 100 ms after the last absent stamp t_(j+1) - 10 ms. Windows by row stamp: 50 to 10 ms before ([-50, -10] ms), 0 to 30 ms after ([0, 30] ms).
- Burst traces: mean over events of the per-event ratio in each 10 ms bin of stamp minus onset (bins [-50, -40) to [90, 100) ms); ECEI and BES levels are channel means of the 10 ms snapshots.
- Raw-stream test discharges: each assigned to the first split whose test files contain it (B.37 part S).
- Blips: the B.35 event rule (five 10 ms steps before, at least three measured T_i of the six rows before); ratios averaged over scored rows of the row table.
