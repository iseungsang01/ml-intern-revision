# B.76 — MAST: the estimate under a stale charge-exchange history, with Thomson scattering of the present at the same radius

Registered: `ces_prediction/experiments/PREREGISTRATION_B76.md`; implementation choices in `ces_prediction/experiments/PREREGISTRATION_B76_ADDENDUM1.md`, written before any readout. CPU only; no network trained or run. Public FAIR-MAST files on disk; T_i of the charge-exchange chords at the pedestal top (1.5 to 5 cm inside the edge of the Thomson density profile). Exploratory on used data (registration section 1): both halves of the shot list were read in the earlier MAST batches with a one-frame history; the availability rules, the anchor inputs, the level target and the validation designs are new on MAST.

Conventions. Skill of A over B = 1 - SSE(A) / SSE(B) on identical rows. Increment = TX over T0 under the same rule on the same rows. Each comparison: point, then 95 % intervals with shots resampled (s) and with sessions (calendar days) resampled (S), 20,000 draws (M1: a re-partition drawn first; M2: an assignment drawn first; M3, M4: the scored units). Verdicts on the session form. Points under M1 and M2 = mean over the three re-partitions or assignments; RMSE there = mean of the per-split RMSE. Every rule is fitted and scored on the common set of its target (rows complete under all six rules), so that the rules differ only in the availability of the history. RMSE in eV. Checks: `docs/paper/b76_checks.md`.

Order of work. Before this readout, these results existed: the four checks of registration section 12 and the counts of the shots and row sets. No MSE, skill or statement under any rule had been computed.

## Limits (registration section 11, unchanged)

- Exploratory on used data (registration section 1).
- MAST has no beam-modulation latency; the delays are imposed by us. The headline delay of 100 ms is a choice, fixed beforehand; MAST discharges are short, so the common set drops the first 150 ms and more of every chord's record.
- Thomson scattering on MAST has one to two samples per frame and reported errors of its own; RT divides two of them.
- The pedestal-top band is defined from the Thomson density profile, the same instrument that supplies the inputs of TX.
- The following level is built from reported values; an error of the analysis that lasts 25 ms or more is in the target too.
- One estimator per rule; no estimator fitted once for all ages.
