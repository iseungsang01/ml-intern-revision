## 9. Notes

- Positive lag = the driver increment comes first. A frame stamp is taken as the centre of its 5 ms exposure; the convention is not in the metadata (half a frame of uncertainty).
- Target increments are centred per chord and driver increments per shot over their valid samples, not over the set of pairs of each lag.
- The circular-shift null rotates the driver increments of each confirmation shot on its own frame grid by a random offset of at least 21 frames; it is computed for the increments in population G only.
- Shots whose block holds a frame interval outside 5.0 +- 0.1 ms or a non-finite stamp are excluded (status irregular_times counts target-population cells that hit the rule in a shot with no usable cell).
- Charge exchange exists only while the viewed beam is on: beam power, the existence of the measurement and its fit quality are tied, so a beam association is not only a plasma response.
